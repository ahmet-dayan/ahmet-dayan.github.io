Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

In short

  • Everything you enter is stored on your phone. It is never sent to us, and we cannot see it. If you want, you can also save backups to your own Google Drive (see section 4); those backups never reach us either.
  • No account is needed. We do not run servers.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. We do not sell or share your data. (One exception: if you buy Premium, your subscription status is verified through RevenueCat with an ID that does not identify you; see section 5.)
  • Files (backup, spreadsheet, pediatrician report) leave your phone only when you send them.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the Baby Hours mobile app (the "App"), developed by Ahmet Dayan ("we", "us"). Our contact details are at the end of this policy.

2. Information you enter

While using the App, you enter:

  • Your baby's name, date of birth (or due date if not yet born), sex (optional), feeding method and the look of the baby character in the App
  • Feeding, nursing, pumping, solid food, sleep, diaper, tummy time and daily routine logs, with their times, durations and amounts
  • Growth measurements and temperature, medicine, vaccine, teething, symptom and doctor visit logs
  • Milestones, memories and diary notes, and any photos you choose to add to them
  • Notes you add to logs and your App settings (reminders, language, theme)

This information is stored in the App's database on your phone. It is not transmitted to us; we cannot see, read or use it. The App's insights, sleep estimates and pediatrician report are calculated entirely on your phone.

Some of these logs may be health-related. Because they never leave your device, we do not process them.

3. Information we do not collect

When you add a photo, only the photo you pick is copied into the App's folder on your phone; the rest of your photo library is not accessed. Reminders are scheduled with your phone's own notification system, without a server. Sleep sounds, stories, learning cards and games are built into the App (only story narration in some languages is downloaded the first time you listen; see section 5); the microphone is not used. Read-aloud in learning cards and games uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine and only reads card names and game instructions (for example, "cat", "Find the cat"). On some phones that engine may use a service from the phone maker or Google/Apple; none of your baby's records are sent to it.

The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, advertising ID, device identifiers, usage analytics or crash reports. The App contains no ads, cookies, trackers or analytics tools.

4. Sharing you start

If you choose, the App creates:

  • A backup file (all babies and logs)
  • A spreadsheet file (CSV)
  • A pediatrician report (PDF)
  • A monthly card image (with a photo you choose and your baby's details)

These files are sent through your phone's share sheet to the destination you choose (for example Google Drive, iCloud Drive, email or your pediatrician). Once a file arrives there, that service's privacy terms apply. Files are not encrypted; keep them somewhere you trust and share them only with people you trust.

When you restore from a backup, the file you pick is read only on your phone.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you connect your Google account on the Family screen, the App writes backups directly to a "Baby Hours" folder in your Google Drive (manually or, if you turn it on, automatically once a day; the last 14 days are kept). For this the App asks Google only for access to files the App itself creates (drive.file): it cannot see any other files in your Drive. Backups travel from your phone to Google over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and never pass through us or any other server; we cannot access them. The connected account's email address is stored only on your phone, to show it on the card. Files in your Drive are governed by Google's terms. You can disconnect at any time and delete the files from your Drive.

Baby Hours' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This information is used only to back up and restore your data; it is never used for advertising, sold, shared with anyone or read by humans.

5. Purchases

Premium purchases are made through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). We never see your payment details. To verify your subscription we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA): the App sends RevenueCat a randomly generated user ID that does not identify you and the store's purchase record (product, date, subscription status). Your name, email and none of your baby's logs are ever sent to RevenueCat. This information is used only to provide Premium, never for ads or tracking. Details: RevenueCat privacy policy. Payments are governed by Apple's and Google's privacy policies.

Story audio and update check

Spanish, German and Portuguese story narration is downloaded from our website (GitHub Pages, ahmet-dayan.github.io) the first time you listen to a book, and kept on your phone. The App may also look up the store's public version information to tell you about a new version. These requests carry no information about you or your baby; as with any internet request, the server (GitHub, Apple) sees your phone's IP address.

6. Your phone's own backups

If iCloud Backup (iPhone) or Google backup (Android) is turned on, your operating system may also back up the App's data. This depends on your phone settings and is governed by Apple's and Google's terms; we have no access to those backups.

7. Children's privacy

The App is designed for parents and caregivers aged 18 or older. It is not directed to children and does not collect information from children. Information about your baby is entered by a parent or caregiver and stays on the phone.

8. Retention and deletion

Your information stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete a single log from the History screen, or everything permanently with Family › Delete all data. Uninstalling the App also deletes its data from your phone. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover deleted data, so please keep backups of records that matter to you. Backups you saved to Google Drive are in your account; "Delete all data" does not remove them, you delete them in Drive.

9. Security

The security of your data largely depends on the security of your phone. We recommend using a screen lock and storing exported files in secure places.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, Turkey's KVKK, or US state laws such as California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Since your data is on your phone rather than with us, you can exercise these rights directly in the App: view, edit, export and delete your logs. You can also contact us with any questions.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

Consumer health data (for example Washington's My Health My Data Act): we do not collect, process or share consumer health data; any health-related logs stay on your device.

11. Changes to this policy

If we ever add a feature that collects data (for example crash reporting or analytics), we will update this policy before doing so and let you know in the App. The current version is always on this page.
